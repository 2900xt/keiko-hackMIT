# Hydrophone potting cup

Lost-mold cup for potting three 20 mm piezo discs (Cheerock leaded discs, wired in parallel)
in epoxy or hot glue. The PLA is a mold, not a housing; the resin is the seal.

- `hydrophone_cup.scad` — OpenSCAD source (50 mm ID, 35 mm tall, 1.2 mm walls)
- `hydrophone_cup.stl` — export
- `hydrophone_cup_A1.3mf` — sliced for Bambu A1, 0.4 nozzle, PLA, 0.28 mm: **19 min, 12 g**, no supports

Features: floor standoffs so resin flows under the discs, inner pour-line ridge at 25 mm,
three rim tabs with 3 mm holes for tying off the cable, rim notch for the cable during the pour.

Pour: 2–3 mm floor layer, let it gel, discs ceramic-side down on the standoffs, cable tied to a
tab, fill to the pour line, then top off. Keep resin over the disc face under 3 mm.
