## Key observations
- The largest openly licensed, call-level annotated sets are: DCLDE 2027 killer whale (>225k boxes, CC BY-NC-ND), AcousticTrends_BlueFinLibrary (105k Antarctic blue/fin annotations, CC BY), NOAA PIFSC humpback (187k h, weak labels), and DORI (5,298 h weak species labels). Watkins remains the default multi-species classification benchmark (BEANS/AVES/Perch/WhaleNet) despite heterogeneous sample rates.
- NOAA's GCS bucket `noaa-passive-bioacoustic` (DCLDE 2015/2018/2027, SanctSound, PIFSC) is the single most useful download root; DCLDE 2013 lives at St Andrews (CC BY) and NCEI.
- Pinniped and sirenian ML datasets are mostly paper-specific and not publicly released (exceptions: Belize manatee figshare set, ANIMAL-SPOT harbour seal on request); Watkins is the only open source for walrus/harp/bearded seal clips.
- Pages that could not be fetched (403): AIP/JASA articles, Wiley (Schall 2024), Springer (MCAD-MM), ResearchGate, DOSITS, Kaggle model pages; details for those come from search snippets and should be verified before citing numbers.
